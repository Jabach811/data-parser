The unlock · Profile Builder

A few minutes of just talking is the entire input.

Speak the way we'd brief a new hire. Profile Builder handles the transcription, the structure, the vocabulary — and writes the connections back into the brain. No forms. No templates. No fields to fight.

What we don't have to do
  • No templates to fill out
  • No taxonomy to learn first
  • No forms with required fields
  • No tagging discipline to maintain

The hardest part of building a knowledge base is the friction of capture. Profile Builder removes it. The same way we'd explain a process to a new hire over coffee — that's the interface.

The interconnection

Every role on the same network.

Data Consultants feed COMS. TCs feed Prod Support. Quality feeds Management. Every team contributes; every team queries. Insight in one corner becomes leverage everywhere else.

Same engine · two scales

One conversation. An entire network.

A single voice memo seeds nodes that link to every other node already in the graph. One profile is a starting point. The same gesture, repeated across the company, becomes the brain. Same structure, same engine — just more voices feeding it.

The output · the big payoff

Custom reports. Built around us, not the other way around.

The brain already knows the shape of the company — every process, every person, every cross-reference. Pick a format. Tune the tone. Choose the audience profile. Ship on demand. Outputs adapt to us; we don't bend ourselves into a template.

Granular customization

Every dial visible

Tune the tone for leadership, widen the appendix for compliance, or compress the whole thing into a stand-up brief without rebuilding the source.

Always current

Same graph, new output

Reports pull from the brain at generation time, so last quarter and this morning still trace back to the same live structure.

Beautiful by default

Designed, not dumped

Self-extending
